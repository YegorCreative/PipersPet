# First Playable Prototype & Roadmap

## 1. Version 0.1 Structure (First Playable)

The goal of the First Playable Prototype (V0.1) is to prove the core loop: explore, bond with a pet, and build. It focuses on game feel and aesthetics over content volume.

### Scope Requirements
*   **One Playable Map:** A small slice of "The Lost Forest." Needs soft lighting, dense trees, and a small river to prove the visual direction.
*   **Movement System:** Third-person camera with smooth, responsive player controls (run, jump, interact).
*   **One Companion:** The Puppy. It must follow the player, have an idle animation, and a bark animation.
*   **Basic Inventory:** A functional UI where the player can collect "Timber" and "Bones."
*   **Simple Building:** The ability to place a "Wall Panel" and a "Floor Panel" on a grid.
*   **One Enemy Type:** A basic "Corrupted Slime." It approaches the player, and the Puppy automatically attacks it.
*   **One Mission:** An NPC in the forest asks the player to gather 5 Timber to build a doghouse.
*   **Multiplayer Test:** Two players connecting via a hardcoded IP or simple Relay to see each other run around the forest.

### Quality Standards for V0.1
*   Do not use generic asset flips. Even if the models are gray-boxed, the lighting and UI must reflect the premium indie target.
*   The camera must feel console-quality (smooth damping, collision avoidance).

## 2. Development Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation & V0.1 (Months 1-2)
*   **Focus:** Core mechanics, camera, character controllers, and basic voxel building math.
*   **Milestone:** Deliver the V0.1 Prototype as outlined above.

### Phase 2: Pet Systems & AI Expansion (Months 3-4)
*   **Focus:** Deepening the companion bond. Implement the Fox and Parrot.
*   **Features:** Skill trees, loyalty metrics, feeding/cooking mechanics, and complex pet pathfinding.
*   **Milestone:** Pet System Prototype (Pets actively engaging in exploration and combat).

### Phase 3: World Building & Content (Months 5-7)
*   **Focus:** Building out "Chapter 1."
*   **Features:** Expanding the voxel catalog (hundreds of building pieces), designing the Spawn Village, setting up hidden caves, and implementing the first Boss Arena.
*   **Milestone:** Chapter 1 Alpha.

### Phase 4: Multiplayer & UI Polish (Months 8-9)
*   **Focus:** Network stability and AAA presentation.
*   **Features:** The Join Code system, Co-op UI, trading system, and finalizing the glassmorphism UI/UX design.
*   **Milestone:** Co-op Beta Testing.

### Phase 5: Optimization & Launch Prep (Months 10-12)
*   **Focus:** Bug fixing, performance tuning, and marketing assets.
*   **Features:** Voxel mesh optimization, LODs, save system hardening.
*   **Milestone:** Early Access / 1.0 Release.
