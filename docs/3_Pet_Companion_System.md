# Pet Companion System

## 1. System Overview

Pets are the emotional core and primary progression mechanic of **Paws of Adventure**. They are not just vanity followers; they are deeply integrated into gameplay, combat, and exploration. The system is designed to create a strong emotional bond between the player and their companions.

### Core Behaviors
*   **Following & Pathfinding:** Pets dynamically follow the player. They will jump over obstacles, swim if needed, or teleport back to the player if they get stuck.
*   **Emotions & Animations:** Pets react to the world. A puppy will bark at butterflies, a fox will sleep when the player goes AFK, and a parrot will dance if the player plays an instrument. If a pet takes damage, it whimpers; if it is healed, it shows a heart emote.
*   **Defending the Player:** Pets automatically engage hostile enemies that attack the player, using their unique combat skills.
*   **Gathering & Interaction:** Pets can be commanded to fetch distant items, dig up buried treasure, or interact with specific mission objectives.

## 2. Companion Designs (Chapter 1)

### The Puppy (Starter Companion)
*   **Role:** Tank / Bruiser / Gatherer
*   **Visual:** Fluffy, expressive voxel dog with floppy ears.
*   **Key Skills:**
    *   *Bark (Active):* Taunts enemies in a radius.
    *   *Dig (Exploration):* Can uncover buried treasure chests in the forest.
    *   *Bite (Passive):* High single-target physical damage.
*   **Evolution:** Grows from a small pup to a majestic, armored hound.

### The Forest Fox
*   **Role:** Agile DPS / Stealth / Scout
*   **Visual:** Sleek, bright orange/white voxel fox with a large, sweeping tail.
*   **Key Skills:**
    *   *Pounce (Active):* Leaps long distances to ambush enemies.
    *   *Scout (Exploration):* Temporarily reveals enemies and rare resources on the minimap.
    *   *Evasion (Passive):* High dodge chance.
*   **Evolution:** Gains mystical elements (e.g., glowing rune markings, a second tail).

### The Macaw Parrot
*   **Role:** Support / Healer / Aerial 
*   **Visual:** Vibrant, colorful voxel bird that sits on the player's shoulder or flies alongside.
*   **Key Skills:**
    *   *Healing Song (Active):* Casts an AoE heal over time for the player and allies.
    *   *Item Fetch (Exploration):* Can fly to out-of-reach ledges to grab items.
    *   *Wind Gust (Passive):* Occasionally knocks back approaching enemies.
*   **Evolution:** Plumage becomes more radiant, eventually glowing with magical light.

## 3. Progression & Mechanics

### Rarity System
Pets found in the wild or hatched from eggs have different rarities. Higher rarity pets have slightly better base stats and unique cosmetic variations (e.g., a "Mythic" fox might be cosmic-colored).
*   *Common (White)* -> *Uncommon (Green)* -> *Rare (Blue)* -> *Epic (Purple)* -> *Legendary (Gold)* -> *Mythic (Prismatic)*

### Leveling System & Skill Tree
*   **Gaining XP:** Pets gain XP through combat, completing missions alongside the player, and training at player-built arenas.
*   **Skill Tree:** Every 5 levels, the player can unlock a new node on the pet's skill tree. Players must choose between branching paths (e.g., spec the Puppy into a pure Tank or a high-damage Bruiser).
*   **Visual Evolution:** At level 15 and 30, pets evolve, growing slightly larger and gaining more complex visual details.

### Loyalty & Feeding Mechanics
*   **Loyalty System:** A separate metric from XP. Loyalty increases through petting, playing with toys, and feeding the pet their favorite foods. High loyalty increases critical hit chance and reduces skill cooldowns.
*   **Feeding:** Pets have a hunger meter. Players must cook or gather specific treats (Bones, Berries, Fish). Feeding a pet grants temporary buffs.
*   **Training:** Players can build training equipment (agility poles, target dummies) at their base. Active mini-games at these stations grant massive XP boosts.

### Pet Inventory & Cosmetics
*   **Backpacks:** Certain pets (like the hound) can be equipped with saddlebags, expanding the player's inventory.
*   **Cosmetics:** Players can craft or buy hats, collars, bandanas, and armor for their pets, allowing for deep customization.
