# World Design & Building System

## 1. World Design overview

The world of **Paws of Adventure** is built using a modular, stylized voxel system. Environments are designed to encourage exploration, reward curiosity, and provide beautiful backdrops for building homes and bonding with pets. 

### Chapter 1: The Lost Forest

**The Lost Forest** serves as the introductory chapter, easing players into the mechanics of exploration, pet rescuing, and basic building. It is a massive, handcrafted (or beautifully generated) biome characterized by towering ancient trees, glowing flora, and winding rivers.

#### Key Locations

*   **Spawn Village (The Grove):** A safe haven where players begin. Features basic vendor NPCs, the first crafting stations, and a serene atmosphere. No enemies can spawn here.
*   **Pet Training Area:** An open, fenced-in meadow near the village designed specifically for practicing pet commands (fetch, sit, stay) and testing combat dummies.
*   **Animal Rescue Areas:** Specific glades or trapped enclosures scattered throughout the forest where players can find and rescue their first companions. These areas often require solving a small puzzle or defeating low-level enemies to unlock the cage.
*   **Winding Rivers & Waterfalls:** High-quality water shaders create beautiful rivers cutting through the forest, leading to hidden grottos and fishing spots.
*   **Hidden Caves:** Tunnels leading underground. The entrance might be hidden behind a waterfall or a breakable voxel wall. Inside, players find glowing crystals (magic resources), rare ores, and slightly tougher enemies.
*   **The Whispering Mountains:** The borders of the map leading to Chapter 2. Features steep climbs, snowy peaks, and the domain of the first flying pets (owls, eagles).
*   **Enemy Zones (The Brambles):** Corrupted patches of the forest where hostile creatures (e.g., shadow-slimes, corrupted boars) roam. The color palette shifts to darker, sickly tones here.
*   **Chapter 1 Boss Arena:** A massive, ancient stump in the deepest part of the forest. The arena is circular, allowing for dodge mechanics and pet-coordination against the "Bramble King."
*   **Multiplayer Campfire Areas:** Designated scenic spots (overlooks, beaches) where players can place a campfire. When friends join, this acts as a fast-travel point and a place to rest, heal, and trade.

## 2. The Building System

The building system takes inspiration from Minecraft's voxel freedom but drastically simplifies the UX to feel "easier and cleaner," focusing on creating beautiful structures without tedious block-by-block micro-management where unnecessary.

### Core Mechanics

*   **Grid & Freeform:** While the world is voxel-based, players can choose to snap to the grid (for walls, floors) or use freeform placement for furniture and decorations (beds, pet bowls).
*   **Prefab Blueprints:** Instead of placing 50 wooden blocks to make a simple hut, players can craft "Wall Panels," "Roof Slopes," and "Fences." This speeds up building and ensures everything looks structurally sound and aesthetically pleasing.
*   **Smart Snapping:** Walls automatically connect to floors, and roofs snap together to form seamless peaks. 
*   **Resource Gathering:** Gathering is satisfying and snappy. Hitting a tree with an axe yields "Timber" rather than raw log blocks, which is then used in the building menu.

### What Players Can Build

*   **Player Homes:** Cozy cabins, multi-story houses, or sprawling mansions. Customizable with wallpapers, lighting, and furniture.
*   **Pet Shelters:** Specialized buildings for companions. Doghouses, bird perches, fox dens, and large barns for bigger creatures. Shelters increase pet happiness and resting XP.
*   **Farms:** Planter boxes for growing crops, berries, and specific herbs used to cook pet treats.
*   **Training Arenas:** Obstacle courses (hoops to jump through, agility poles) that players can build to actively level up their pets' speed and stamina.
*   **Custom Villages:** In multiplayer, friends can link their plots together to build massive, shared towns.
*   **Storage Systems:** Visually distinct chests, barrels, and display racks to organize gathered loot and rare items.
